These small, variable local samples do not establish a performance improvement. All 18 light-theme LHRs identify the hero lead text as LCP. The EN after sample 2 lists a 31 KB render-blocking CSS request, 103 KB private and 80 KB Inter font preloads, and 229 KB/159 KB JS chunks; it attributes a 248 ms long task to the larger chunk. The observed LCP insight timings differ from the simulated Lighthouse metric.

The genuine public-fallback export was measured separately with the same transport and settings, three cold light-theme samples per locale. Median performance scores were EN 79, DE 79 and FA 81; median LCP was 4.78, 4.79 and 4.74 seconds, and median TBT 233, 180 and 143 ms respectively. All nine accessibility scores were 100. Individual samples varied sharply, including one FA TBT of 5.1 seconds. There is no current-source public-fallback before baseline, and no speedup is claimed. EN and DE asset manifests also changed between the private and fallback exports, including removal of the private-font preload, so neither locale is treated as having unchanged delivery. Raw LHRs, settings and medians: `/private/tmp/lumo-customer-journey-2026-10-18/performance/fallback-after/`; asset manifests: `/private/tmp/lumo-customer-journey-2026-10-18/network-{private,fallback}.json`.

These local measurements used the Python HTTP/1.0 preview, without production compression or cache behavior. They are comparable to one another under that local transport, but do not predict deployed or field performance; any fallback comparison must use the same transport.
